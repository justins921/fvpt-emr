## About This Privacy Policy

Sobojinski Solutions, LLC ("Sobojinski Solutions," "we," "us," or "our") operates the EMR OS platform, a cloud-based electronic medical records system designed specifically for physical therapy practices. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect health information in connection with the EMR OS platform.

EMR OS is used by physical therapy clinics, practices, and healthcare providers ("Covered Entities" or "Providers") to manage patient records, clinical documentation, scheduling, billing, and related healthcare operations. In this capacity, Sobojinski Solutions acts as a **Business Associate** under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA"), as amended by the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act ("HITECH Act"), and the regulations promulgated thereunder (collectively, the "HIPAA Rules," codified at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164).

Your physical therapy provider is the **Covered Entity** responsible for your healthcare and your health information. Your provider's own Notice of Privacy Practices governs how your provider uses and discloses your health information. This Privacy Policy describes Sobojinski Solutions' practices as the technology provider that processes health information on behalf of your provider.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Protected Health Information (PHI)

In the course of providing EMR OS services to physical therapy Providers, we may process the following categories of Protected Health Information on their behalf:

**Patient Demographic Information**
- Full name (first, middle, last)
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Home address, city, state, ZIP code
- Telephone number(s) (home, mobile, work)
- Email address
- Social Security number (where required for billing or insurance purposes)

- Emergency contact information
- Preferred language and communication preferences

**Insurance and Financial Information**
- Health insurance plan name(s) and policy/group numbers
- Insurance subscriber information
- Medicare/Medicaid identification numbers
- Workers' compensation claim information
- Billing account numbers
- Payment and copay information
- Prior authorization numbers and details
- Coordination of benefits information

**Clinical and Treatment Information**
- Medical record number (assigned by Provider)
- Referring physician name and contact information
- Primary care physician information
- Diagnoses and diagnostic codes (ICD-10)
- Procedure codes (CPT)
- Physical therapy evaluation findings (range of motion, strength, functional assessments, pain scales)
- Treatment plans and goals
- Daily treatment notes and progress notes
- Discharge summaries
- Exercise and home program prescriptions
- Functional outcome measures
- Imaging and lab results (if uploaded)
- Medication lists (as relevant to physical therapy care)
- Allergy information
- Surgical history and relevant medical history
- Patient-reported outcome measures

**Scheduling and Communication Records**
- Appointment dates, times, and types
- Appointment status (scheduled, completed, cancelled, no-show)
- Appointment reminders sent (with consent)
- Secure messages between patient and Provider (if applicable)
- Telehealth session records (if applicable)

### 1.2 Technical and Usage Information

In addition to PHI processed on behalf of Providers, we collect the following technical information related to the use of EMR OS:

- Internet Protocol (IP) addresses and approximate geolocation (city/region level)
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Device type and identifiers
- Login timestamps and session duration
- Pages accessed and features used within EMR OS
- Error logs and performance data

This technical information is used to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the EMR OS platform. Where this information can be associated with an identifiable individual's health information, it is treated as PHI and protected accordingly.

Sobojinski Solutions processes PHI solely on behalf of and at the direction of the physical therapy Providers who use EMR OS. We do not independently determine the purposes for which PHI is used. Our use of PHI is governed by our Business Associate Agreements with each Provider and is limited to the following purposes:

### 2.1 Providing EMR OS Services

- Hosting and delivering the EMR OS platform to Providers
- Storing and maintaining electronic health records
- Processing clinical documentation, scheduling, and billing data
- Generating reports requested by Providers
- Enabling secure communication features
- Providing data backup and disaster recovery services

### 2.2 Treatment, Payment, and Healthcare Operations Support

On behalf of Providers, EMR OS facilitates:

- **Treatment:** Enabling Providers to document patient evaluations, create treatment plans, record treatment sessions, track patient progress, and coordinate care with referring physicians and other healthcare providers.

- **Payment:** Enabling Providers to submit insurance claims, manage billing, process patient statements, verify insurance eligibility, obtain prior authorizations, and manage accounts receivable.

- **Healthcare Operations:** Enabling Providers to conduct quality assessment and improvement activities, manage practice operations, train staff, conduct audits, and perform other activities permitted under 45 CFR 164.501.

### 2.3 Platform Operations and Security

- Monitoring system performance, availability, and security
- Detecting and preventing unauthorized access, fraud, and abuse
- Conducting security assessments and audits
- Maintaining audit logs as required by HIPAA (45 CFR 164.312(b))
- Resolving technical issues and providing customer support
- Performing data backup and disaster recovery operations

### 2.4 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

- Complying with HIPAA and other applicable laws and regulations
- Responding to lawful requests from regulatory authorities
- Fulfilling obligations under Business Associate Agreements
- Supporting Providers in responding to patient rights requests

### 2.5 What We Do NOT Do With Your Health Information

Sobojinski Solutions does **not**:

- **Sell your health information** to any third party for any purpose
- **Use your health information for marketing** purposes without explicit authorization
- **Use your health information for advertising**, profiling, or targeted content
- **Share your health information with data brokers** or consumer reporting agencies
- **Use your health information to make employment, credit, or insurance decisions**
- **Mine your health information for commercial purposes** unrelated to providing EMR OS services
- **Access your health information** except as necessary to provide, maintain, and secure the EMR OS platform

## 4. Your Rights Regarding Your Health Information

Under HIPAA, you have specific rights regarding your health information. Because your physical therapy Provider is the Covered Entity responsible for your care, most rights requests should be directed to your Provider. Sobojinski Solutions will cooperate with your Provider to fulfill these requests.

### 4.1 Right to Access Your Health Information

**What this means:** You have the right to inspect and obtain a copy of your health information maintained in a Designated Record Set, as defined in 45 CFR 164.524.

**How to exercise this right:** Submit a written request to your physical therapy Provider. Your Provider may direct us to provide the information to you, and we will do so in the electronic form and format you request, if readily producible.

**Timeframe:** Your Provider must respond to your request within 30 days (which may be extended by an additional 30 days with written notice, per 45 CFR 164.524(b)(2)).

**Fees:** Your Provider may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee for copies, as permitted by 45 CFR 164.524(c)(4).

### 4.2 Right to Request Amendment

**What this means:** You have the right to request that your Provider amend your health information if you believe it is inaccurate or incomplete, as provided in 45 CFR 164.526.

**How to exercise this right:** Submit a written request to your physical therapy Provider, specifying the information you believe is inaccurate and the reason for the requested change. If your Provider approves the amendment, we will update the information in EMR OS.

**Timeframe:** Your Provider must respond within 60 days (which may be extended by an additional 30 days with written notice).

**Note:** Your Provider may deny the request under certain circumstances specified in 45 CFR 164.526(a)(2), such as if the information was not created by the Provider, is not part of the Designated Record Set, or is accurate and complete.

### 4.3 Right to an Accounting of Disclosures

**What this means:** You have the right to receive a list of certain disclosures of your health information made by your Provider or on your Provider's behalf, as provided in 45 CFR 164.528.

**How to exercise this right:** Submit a written request to your physical therapy Provider. The accounting covers the six (6) years prior to the request (or a shorter period if specified).

**Exclusions:** The accounting does not include disclosures made for treatment, payment, or healthcare operations; disclosures made to you or with your authorization; or certain other categories specified in 45 CFR 164.528(a)(1).

**Timeframe:** Your Provider must provide the accounting within 60 days (which may be extended by an additional 30 days with written notice).

**Fees:** The first accounting in any 12-month period is provided at no charge. Your Provider may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee for subsequent requests.

### 4.4 Right to Request Restrictions

**What this means:** You have the right to request that your Provider restrict certain uses and disclosures of your health information, as provided in 45 CFR 164.522(a).

**How to exercise this right:** Submit a written request to your physical therapy Provider, describing the restriction you are requesting.

**Note:** Your Provider is not required to agree to most restriction requests. However, your Provider **must** agree to a restriction on disclosures to a health plan for payment or healthcare operations purposes if the disclosure relates to a service for which you have paid out of pocket in full (42 U.S.C. 17935(a); 45 CFR 164.522(a)(1)(vi)).

### 4.5 Right to Confidential Communications

**What this means:** You have the right to request that your Provider communicate with you about health matters in a certain way or at a certain location, as provided in 45 CFR 164.522(b).

**How to exercise this right:** Submit a request to your physical therapy Provider (e.g., requesting that appointment reminders be sent to a specific phone number or email address rather than your home address).

### 4.6 Right to Receive Notification of a Breach

**What this means:** You have the right to receive notification if your unsecured health information is subject to a Breach, as defined under 45 CFR 164.402 and required by 45 CFR 164.404.

**How this works:** If a Breach of your unsecured health information occurs, your physical therapy Provider (as the Covered Entity) will notify you in writing within 60 days of discovery. If Sobojinski Solutions discovers a Breach, we will promptly notify your Provider so they can fulfill their notification obligations.

### 4.7 Right to a Paper Copy of This Notice

You may request a paper copy of this Privacy Policy at any time by contacting us at the address listed in Section 7.

### 4.8 Right to File a Complaint

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may:

- File a complaint with your physical therapy Provider's Privacy Officer
- File a complaint with Sobojinski Solutions' Privacy Officer (see Section 7)
- File a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights:
  - **Online:** [https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/filing-a-complaint/index.html](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/filing-a-complaint/index.html)
  - **Phone:** 1-800-368-1019
  - **Mail:** Centralized Case Management Operations, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, 200 Independence Avenue SW, Room 509F HHH Bldg., Washington, D.C. 20201

**You will not be retaliated against for filing a complaint.**

## 5. Security Measures

Sobojinski Solutions implements comprehensive administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of health information in compliance with the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C).

### 5.1 Administrative Safeguards

- **Security Management Process:** We maintain a documented risk analysis and risk management program (45 CFR 164.308(a)(1))
- **Workforce Security:** Employees and contractors undergo background checks and are granted access to PHI only as necessary for their job functions, following the principle of least privilege (45 CFR 164.308(a)(3))
- **Security Awareness and Training:** All personnel with access to PHI receive HIPAA security awareness training upon hire and annually thereafter (45 CFR 164.308(a)(5))
- **Incident Response:** We maintain and regularly test a Security Incident Response Plan for identifying, responding to, and mitigating security incidents (45 CFR 164.308(a)(6))
- **Business Associate Management:** We maintain Business Associate Agreements with all subcontractors who access PHI and monitor their compliance (45 CFR 164.308(b))

### 5.2 Physical Safeguards

- **Data Center Security:** EMR OS infrastructure is hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers with 24/7 physical security, biometric access controls, surveillance, and environmental controls (45 CFR 164.310(a))
- **Workstation Security:** Employee workstations are encrypted, managed, and subject to security policies including automatic screen lock and remote wipe capability (45 CFR 164.310(b)-(c))
- **Device and Media Controls:** Policies and procedures govern the receipt, movement, and disposal of hardware and electronic media containing PHI (45 CFR 164.310(d))

### 5.3 Technical Safeguards

- **Access Controls:** Role-based access controls ensure users can access only the minimum PHI necessary for their function. Multi-factor authentication is required for administrative access (45 CFR 164.312(a))
- **Audit Controls:** Comprehensive audit logging records user access, modifications, and system events. Logs are monitored, retained, and reviewed regularly (45 CFR 164.312(b))
- **Integrity Controls:** Mechanisms are in place to protect PHI from improper alteration or destruction, including checksums, version control, and database integrity monitoring (45 CFR 164.312(c))
- **Transmission Security:** All PHI transmitted over networks is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. No unencrypted transmission of PHI is permitted (45 CFR 164.312(e))
- **Encryption at Rest:** All PHI stored in databases, file systems, and backups is encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent encryption (rendering PHI "unusable, unreadable, or indecipherable" per HHS guidance on Unsecured PHI, 45 CFR 164.402)
- **Vulnerability Management:** Regular vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, and patching processes are maintained
- **Backup and Recovery:** Automated, encrypted backups are performed regularly with tested restoration procedures to ensure data availability

### 5.4 Organizational Standards

- **Privacy and Security Policies:** We maintain comprehensive written policies and procedures that are reviewed and updated annually
- **Risk Assessments:** We conduct comprehensive risk assessments at least annually and whenever significant changes occur to our systems or operations
- **Third-Party Assessments:** We engage independent security assessors to evaluate our security posture
- **Documentation Retention:** All HIPAA-required documentation is retained for a minimum of six (6) years (45 CFR 164.530(j))

## 6. Data Retention and Disposal

### 6.1 Retention

Sobojinski Solutions retains PHI in EMR OS for the duration of our service relationship with the Provider, plus any retention period required by applicable law or our Business Associate Agreement. Providers control retention decisions for their patient records within EMR OS.

### 6.2 Data Export

Upon request or upon termination of the service relationship, Providers may export their data from EMR OS in standard, interoperable formats.

### 6.3 Disposal

Upon termination of a Provider's account, and after the applicable data export and retention period, PHI is securely destroyed in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 guidelines for media sanitization. We provide written certification of data destruction upon request.

## 10. Special Populations and Information Types

### 10.1 Minors

Health information of minors is subject to additional protections under certain state laws. Access to a minor's health information through EMR OS is controlled by the Provider in accordance with applicable state and federal law governing parental access and the rights of minors.

### 10.2 Deceased Individuals

HIPAA protections for health information continue for 50 years following the death of the individual (45 CFR 160.103). Health information of deceased patients in EMR OS is subject to the same protections as living patients during this period.

### 10.3 Personal Representatives

A personal representative (such as a legal guardian, healthcare power of attorney, or executor of an estate) may exercise the rights of an individual with respect to PHI, in accordance with 45 CFR 164.502(g) and applicable state law. Verification of personal representative status is the responsibility of the Provider.
